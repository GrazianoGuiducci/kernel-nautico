## Boundaries

Domain source remains distinct from:

- company confidential state;
- company-local competence;
- one vessel's identity;
- execution authority;
- product safety/control systems.

## Value relation

The public kernel provides a domain foundation.

The enterprise incarnation creates value by becoming situated in the actual
organization.

The vessel instance, when useful, continues that relation into product life
without collapsing product and company identity.
