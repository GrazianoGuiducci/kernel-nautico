company CRM for significant service issues. It carries detailed failure context,
priority, components, part/serial identifiers and other evidence. The warranty
manager passes it to the product improvement manager, who activates the
competent parties. Corrective/preventive actions are selected according to
failure type and impact; effectiveness is checked; recurrence opens a linked
report with the causal cluster "ineffective solution".

New Product Development procedure, where later design reviews or other
interventions may follow.
