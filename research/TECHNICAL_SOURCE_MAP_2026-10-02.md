### Historical production-cycle disclosure

Ferretti listing prospectus:
https://www.ferrettigroup.com/portals/4/Investor-relations/Listing-documents/Prospectus/E_886756_FSPA_0320_1200_ESS.pdf

Useful composite cycle:

~~~text
hull production
-> systems/machinery + below-deck assembly
-> superstructure assembly
-> furnishings assembly
-> launch and sea trials
~~~

Useful alloy/full-custom relations include:
preliminary feasibility, detailed design, hydrodynamics/stability/trim,
class verification, block fabrication, machinery, electrical/water/HVAC,
interiors/painting/finish and final testing.

Treat this as historical company disclosure supporting a general process model,
not as proof that every present Ferretti line uses an identical sequence.

## European Union

### Recreational Craft Directive 2013/53/EU

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2013/53/oj/eng

Scope relation:
recreational craft intended for sports/leisure with hull length 2.5–24 m are
within the Directive's definition/scope, with conformity procedures dependent
on design category and length.

Do not project this profile onto larger or differently used yachts.
