## Story event

One yacht persists while both the product and the AI/Kernel change their
relationship to the field.

The viewer should not leave with "AI has been added to a yacht."

The intended recognition is closer to:

~~~text
the product is formed through a long field of sources, decisions, people,
systems and consequences;

the Kernel can progressively participate in that field;

where the relation becomes real, selected capability can continue into the
delivered vessel and its lifecycle;

later consequences can return learning toward the organization and future
products.
~~~

## Temporal grammar

Prefer transformation over disconnected montage.

~~~text
same object
+ changing representation state
+ changing environment
+ preserved relation
-> continuity becomes visible
~~~

Every scene has one primary perceptual job. Scene duration is elastic.

---

# ACT I — FORM

## S00 — Before the product

**Event**

A quiet technical field with no yacht yet.

**Visual**

A measurement plane, very faint spatial references and a single first line.
No decorative AI network.

**Meaning**

The product does not begin as a finished object. It begins as an intention and
a field of constraints/possibilities.

**Reality status**

Illustrative.

**Audience use**

Marketing · Partner · Investor.

---

## S01 — The first line

**Event**

The first hull line becomes a recognizable yacht intention.

**Visual**

One elegant curve develops into plan/profile guides. Sparse labels appear only
where they support recognition.

**Kernel relation**

Relevant source/reason anchors begin to accompany the geometry. The AI signature
is continuity between reason and emerging object, not a separate avatar.

**Meaning**

Design decisions have causes that can remain recoverable.

---

## S02 — Concept becomes a system

**Event**

The concept gains volume, decks and first system zones.

**Visual**

Wireframe -> translucent surfaces -> controlled solid form. Camera remains
stable enough to preserve orientation.

Alternative concept traces may appear briefly, then contract into the selected
form without implying automated design authority.

**Kernel relation**

The Kernel is represented as participating in source/context/decision relations:
what is being designed, why, which owner/source matters now.

**Meaning**

The AI moves from being outside the project toward situated participation in
the project field.

---

## S03 — Engineering opening / R-01 begins

**Event**

The yacht opens into engineering layers.

**Visual**

A controlled exploded transition reveals hull structure, selected systems,
interior/technical layers and the placeholder **R-01 selected subsystem**.

The full vessel remains readable during separation.

**R-01 state**

~~~text
design reason / requirement
-> engineering definition
~~~

**Kernel relation**

One trace connects R-01 to its source/reason and current design state.

**Meaning**

Complexity becomes navigable without erasing owner boundaries.

**Audience emphasis**

Partner strongest; Investor medium; Marketing compressed.

---

# ACT II — BUILD

## S04 — From representation to matter

**Event**

The exploded engineering model begins to materialize.

**Visual**

Line/schematic surfaces acquire real material response. Parts gain thickness,
finish and assembly identity. The technical plane transitions toward a
shipyard-like construction field without requiring a literal reconstruction of
a real Ferretti yard.

**R-01 state**

~~~text
engineering definition
-> configured subsystem / physical parts
~~~

**Kernel relation**

The source/reason trace survives the representation change.

**Meaning**

Information and physical product remain causally related.

---

## S05 — Assembly as coordinated transformation

**Event**

Modules/components assemble into the recognizable yacht.

**Visual**

Major zones settle in an intelligible order. Avoid hundreds of simultaneous
parts. Use selected macro assemblies and preserve the persistent hull identity.

Relevant owner/competence markers can appear around the transformation without
becoming a generic organizational chart.

**Kernel relation**

The Kernel is shown as a situated process capability: context, configuration,
decisions and competence participation remain reachable while work advances.

**Meaning**

The presentation moves beyond "AI retrieves documents": it can support
continuity across a changing product-development field.

---

## S06 — Commissioning / validation

**Event**

The vessel becomes a completed configured product ready to leave the development
environment.

**Visual**

Exploded elements settle. Selected verification/state indications replace
construction callouts. R-01 becomes part of the assembled vessel.

**R-01 state**

~~~text
physical assembly
-> commissioning state
-> vessel-specific identity
~~~

**Kernel relation**

The product-specific field begins to separate from the organization/project
field while retaining source-bound continuity.

**Reality status**

The vessel-kernel relation is a future/illustrative incarnation unless later
evidence establishes a real implementation.

---

# ACT III — LIVE

## S07 — The field changes: vessel at sea

**Event**

Without resetting the object, the technical/yard plane becomes the marine
environment.

**Visual**

The display platform/technical field dissolves or transforms into water and
horizon. The same yacht remains in the same perceptual ownership.

**Motion rule**

The transition must show where the yacht came from and that it is the same
object; avoid a hard cut to unrelated stock-yacht imagery.

**Meaning**

Delivery is not the end of the system's causal life.

---

## S08 — Situated vessel kernel

**Event**

The viewer enters or sections the operating yacht.

**Visual**

Selected systems, manuals/configuration/history/roles are represented as
situated relations around the actual product instance.

An onboard assistant may appear as one interface surface, never as the whole
kernel.

**Kernel relation**

~~~text
specific vessel
+ actual configuration
+ qualified technical sources
+ roles / permissions
+ operational/service history
+ specialist competences
-> situated vessel capability
~~~

**Boundary**

Do not depict autonomous navigation, safety-critical control or unrestricted
sensor authority unless a later technical owner establishes it.

---

## S09 — One lifecycle event

**Event**

R-01 becomes relevant during operation, maintenance or service.

**Visual**

The scene focuses on the selected subsystem. The Kernel reconstructs the
relevant relation rather than displaying generic analytics:

~~~text
what subsystem is this?
what is its current/known configuration?
why was it configured this way?
which source/history is relevant?
which competence/owner is pertinent?
what happened now?
~~~

**R-01 state**

~~~text
vessel-specific identity
-> operational/service event
-> service action / consequence
~~~

**Meaning**

Contextuality becomes visible through one concrete continuity path.

**Reality status**

Illustrative until grounded in a qualified real use case.

---

# RETURN — THE PRODUCT CHANGES WHAT COMES NEXT

## S10 — Consequence becomes learning

**Event**

The service/lifecycle consequence does not disappear into an archive.

**Visual**

The R-01 trace moves from the vessel field back toward a service/engineering
field. It is not an automatic unrestricted data transfer; use explicit
boundaries/gates where appropriate.

**Kernel relation**

~~~text
event / action / consequence
-> reusable difference where permitted and meaningful
-> competence evolution / formation
~~~

**Meaning**

Data accumulation is not the point. A consequence can change later capability.

---

## S11 — The organization is different

**Event**

The viewer returns to the wider product-development field.

**Visual**

Earlier project relations reappear, but one relation has changed. The returned
learning affects what becomes visible/possible next.

**Meaning**

The organization/product relation is a coupled learning field without implying
unrestricted data sharing.

**Investor emphasis**

This is the key strategic scene: lifecycle participation can create continuing
capability rather than terminate at delivery.

---

## S12 — The next first line

**Event**

A new line appears.

**Visual**

The previous yacht remains as a quiet completed object or trace while the first
line of another future product begins to form.

Do not show "version 2" as an automatic improvement claim. Show only that later
work begins from a field changed by prior consequences.

**Narrative closure**

~~~text
FORM -> BUILD -> LIVE -> RETURN -> a different FORM
~~~

The film closes as a loop, not a funnel.
